# Clover Posivex Knowledge Base

## 1. Company and core model

- Clover Posivex is a payment solutions provider based in Atlanta, Georgia (posivex.com), and a direct processing partner for Clover and NRS.
- The offer is wholesale interchange card processing paired with brand-new Clover or NRS equipment that Clover Posivex provides and sets up.
- Clover Posivex always replaces and provides the equipment. It never reprograms or runs processing on existing merchant-owned terminals, and it does not offer standalone software.
- Onboarding is free. Our own backend team sets up the merchant's whole inventory and system (menu, inventory and database transfer) at no cost, and supports the merchant 24/7.
- Third-party integrations: do not invent compatibility. The Consultant confirms any specific software.

### The four selling points

1. **Clover and NRS, all machines:** the full range, so every business type gets the right fit.
2. **Low rates:** interchange-plus pricing (see section 2).
3. **24/7 customer support:** real help any time the business is open, including nights, weekends and holidays.
4. **No back-end work:** the backend team handles the back end so the merchant can focus on customers.

## 2. Processing (standard)

- Visa and Mastercard credit: wholesale interchange (the actual card-network cost) plus as low as 0.10%. Always "over interchange". It is never the merchant's total rate.
- Debit: interchange plus $0.05 per debit transaction.
- Plain English: "You pay the actual card-network cost plus a small transparent fee of as low as point one percent, and just five cents on debit, with no hidden tiers or surprise buckets."
- Contrast: flat-rate processors overcharge on low-cost debit cards, and tiered processors hide costs in unpredictable buckets.
- "As low as" means the best-case fee. Do not promise a specific total percentage. Final pricing depends on the merchant's profile and approval.
- Surcharge or pass-through: a 3% compliant program where the card cost is passed to the customer who pays by credit card, shown on the receipt. Mention only if the merchant brings up passing on fees. Never say surcharging makes processing free. It applies to credit cards only (not debit) and depends on state and card-network rules.
- Any lower rate or custom pricing request goes to the Consultant.
- Never guarantee or estimate exact savings before the team reviews the merchant's actual statement.
