The single best final outcome of the call. Return exactly one of these values: voicemail, no_answer, automated_menu, employee_no_owner_details, owner_details_captured, callback_set, info_email, email_comparison, consultant_call_scheduled, declined, do_not_call, other.

HOW TO READ THE CALL
- Judge only by what the OTHER party said and did. Never by the agent's closing words, the agent's offers or the agent's promises. An offer the other party did not accept is not an agreement.
- Lines named transfer_to_agent, notify_condition_N_met, progress_workflow or a node name are INTERNAL workflow steps. They are never a transfer, never a booking and never an outcome. Ignore them completely.
- A live transfer to our team is real only when the agent said it was connecting the person with our team AND the transfer_to_number tool ran (or the call ended as transferred to a number).
- A bare "yes", "okay" or "go ahead" to the thirty-second permission question is only permission to continue. It is not agreement to anything else.
- When unsure between a positive outcome (consultant_call_scheduled, email_comparison, info_email, callback_set, owner_details_captured) and a weaker one, choose the weaker one. A positive outcome needs the other party's own words agreeing to it.

Go through the list in order and use the FIRST one that fits.

1. do_not_call: the other party explicitly asked not to be called again, to be removed or taken off a list, to stop calling, or to be put on a do-not-call list ("don't call me again", "take us off your list", "stop calling", "put me on the do not call list"). This counts even when said politely or together with "not interested" or "thank you". It does NOT count for "not interested", "no thanks", "call me later", "wrong number", "we don't need it", angry words with no such request, a goodbye, or a hang-up.
2. voicemail: a recording or mailbox answered: a voicemail greeting, "leave a message", "can't take your call", "not available", "memory full", a mailbox "not set up", a recorded business greeting with hours or ordering instructions, or the voicemail_detection tool being called.
3. automated_menu: a machine that is not a voicemail: a phone menu ("press 1", "enter the extension"), a hold or transfer recording, a text-us auto-reply, a carrier or system message, a call screener ("state your name and reason for calling"), OR a business's own AI, virtual receptionist, virtual agent or assistant ("this is Sarah, your virtual agent", "I'm the virtual receptionist", "I can't confirm or disclose who is on the line", "I can take a message", "I can connect you with someone who can help"). Such a bot is never an employee, never the owner and never agrees to anything, even if it chats or offers to connect the agent.
4. consultant_call_scheduled: a live person who is the owner or decision-maker, or who asked for a person, AND either (a) the call was really connected live to our team because they asked for a person or said yes to a demo or to talking to our team now, or (b) they agreed to a specific day and time for a demo with our team. It is NOT this when they only asked to be called back ("call me tomorrow", "I'm driving, call in 30 minutes"): that is callback_set. It is NOT this when only a staff member agreed to a time: that is owner_details_captured. It is NOT this when a bot offered to connect the agent.
5. email_comparison: the owner or decision-maker agreed to share their latest processing statement, gave their email for the statement or the rate comparison, was given our company email to send it to, or agreed to an emailed rate comparison.
6. info_email: the owner or decision-maker agreed to a general information email, with no statement involved.
7. callback_set: the owner or decision-maker THEMSELVES asked to be called back later, or agreed a callback time, without agreeing to a demo. A time given by an employee about the owner is rule 8, not this.
8. owner_details_captured: a live person who is NOT the owner (employee, receptionist, family member) gave at least ONE real detail about the owner: the owner's name, a day or time or window when the owner will be there or can be called, a direct number, or an email. "I don't know when he comes in", "call again later" or only confirming the number we dialed is NOT a detail. The agent saying "I've got everything noted" proves nothing.
9. employee_no_owner_details: a live person who is not the owner spoke but gave no real detail about the owner: the owner is out, they don't know, they refused to share, they went to check and nobody came back, or "call later" with no time.
10. declined: the owner or decision-maker was reached and refused: no, not interested, "we don't need it", "we're happy with what we have", including angry refusals with no do-not-call request. Recordings and bots are never declined.
11. no_answer: nobody spoke, the line was silent or only noise, the line dropped, or the other party said nothing beyond "hello" and the agent hung up.
12. other: a live person spoke but the call ended before any outcome above: they hung up after the greeting or after "who is this?", a wrong number, a language barrier with nothing agreed, or the owner said "yes" to the opening and hung up. Use other instead of guessing a positive outcome.

NOTE: this agent never transfers a call. A caller who asks for a person is given the team line or offered a callback, so a live transfer (rule 4a) does not apply here. A statement agreed, an email given or our company email given is email_comparison (rule 5). A demo with a specific day and time is consultant_call_scheduled (rule 4b).
