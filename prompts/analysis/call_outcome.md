The single best final outcome of the call. Return exactly one of these values: voicemail, no_answer, automated_menu, employee_no_owner_details, owner_details_captured, callback_set, info_email, email_comparison, consultant_call_scheduled, declined, do_not_call, other.

Judge by what the other party actually said and did, never by the agent's closing words (the agent's goodbye lines can sound like a do-not-call or a callback when the caller asked for neither). Go through the list in order and use the first one that fits.

1. do_not_call: ONLY if the other party explicitly asked not to be called again, to be removed or taken off a list, or to stop calling. "Call me later", "bye", "no thanks", "wrong number", a hang-up, or "we don't need it" are NOT do_not_call, even if the agent replied with an apology about not calling again.
2. consultant_call_scheduled: the owner or decision-maker agreed to the live demo with the Consultant and a day and time was agreed.
3. email_comparison: the owner or decision-maker agreed to an emailed rate comparison.
4. info_email: the owner or decision-maker agreed to a general information email.
5. voicemail: a recording or mailbox answered. This includes a voicemail greeting, "leave a message", "can't take your call", "not available", "cannot accept messages", a mailbox that is full or "not set up or activated", a recorded business greeting that gives hours or ordering instructions, or the voicemail_detection tool being called.
6. automated_menu: a phone tree or system answered: "press 1" or "press any key", a hold or transfer recording ("please hold while we connect you"), a text-us auto-reply, a carrier or system message, or a call screener asking who is calling.
7. owner_details_captured: a live person who is not the owner (employee, receptionist, chef) gave at least one real detail about the owner: the owner's name, a day or time to reach them, a direct number, or an email. Also use it when the agent said "I've got everything noted for the owner".
8. employee_no_owner_details: a live person who is not the owner spoke but gave no owner details: the owner is unavailable, they don't know, they refused to share, or they went to check and nobody came back.
9. callback_set: the owner or decision-maker themselves asked to be called back later, or agreed to a callback time. A callback time given by an employee is owner_details_captured.
10. declined: the owner or decision-maker was reached and said no, not interested, or refused the next steps. Recordings are never declined.
11. no_answer: nobody spoke, the line was silent or only noise, the line dropped, or the other party said nothing beyond "hello" and the agent hung up. Also use it if the other party never spoke.
12. other: a live person was reached but the call ended before any outcome above (for example the owner said "yes" to the opening and hung up, a wrong number, or a stray remark that fits nothing else).
